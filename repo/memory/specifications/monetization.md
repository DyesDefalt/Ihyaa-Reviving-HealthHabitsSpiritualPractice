# Ihyaa — Monetization & Go-To-Market Plan
## 1\. Business Model
### Freemium + Subscription (Hybrid)
Model: **Free tier yang genuinely useful** + **Premium yang unlocks depth dan personalization**.

Kenapa bukan pure hard paywall? Karena app Islami punya moral dimension. Users expect basic religious content to be free. Paywall yang terlalu agresif akan terasa exploitative.

Kenapa bukan pure freemium? Karena conversion rate freemium median hanya 2.2% (RevenueCat 2025). Butuh premium tier yang compelling.

## 3\. Paywall Strategy
### When to Show Paywall
1. **NOT on first open**. Let user complete at least 1 habit first.
2. **After first "aha moment"**: User completes a habit → sees evidence card → "unlock full citation" prompt
3. **Soft paywall on premium features**: Meal planning, Ramadan mode, family plan → "Upgrade to Pro" prompt
4. **Periodic reminder**: After 7 days of active use, show value summary + upgrade prompt
5. **Ramadan spike**: 2 weeks before Ramadan, targeted campaign "Prepare your best Ramadan"
### Paywall Copy Principles
*   Frame as investment in health + ibadah, not as purchase
*   Show concrete value: "You've completed 45 habits this month. Pro gives you unlimited habits + full evidence."
*   Social proof: "Join 1,000+ Muslims investing in their health"
*   Urgency: Annual plan discount, Ramadan timing
*   No guilt-tripping. No dark patterns.
### Free Trial
*   **14-day free trial** for Premium (longer than 7-day industry standard)
*   Reason: habit formation takes 2+ weeks. 7-day trial ends before user feels the benefit.
*   RevenueCat data: 17-32 day trials convert at 45.7% vs 26.8% for <4 day trials
*   No credit card required for trial (reduces friction, Google Play handles this)

## 5\. Go-To-Market Strategy
### Phase 1: Soft Launch (Month 1-2)
**Target**: 1,000 downloads, 50 paying users
*   Play Store internal testing → closed beta (200 users)
*   Recruit from: Muslim communities on Twitter/X, Instagram, WhatsApp groups
*   Personal network + Muslim tech community
*   Iterate based on feedback, fix critical bugs
*   Goal: prove core loop works, D7 retention >30%
### Phase 2: Public Launch (Month 3-4)
**Target**: 10,000 downloads, 500 paying users
*   Play Store public release
*   Product Hunt launch (target: top 5 product of the day)
*   Muslim tech Twitter/X engagement
*   Instagram/TikTok content marketing:
    *   "5 Sunnah habits that science proves are healthy"
    *   "Why you should drink water like the Prophet ﷺ"
    *   "Thibbun Nabawi vs modern medicine: what they agree on"
*   Influencer outreach: micro-influencers (10K-100K followers) in Muslim lifestyle niche
*   Press kit + outreach to Muslim media outlets
### Phase 3: Ramadan Campaign (Month 5-6, depending on Hijri calendar)
**Target**: 50,000+ downloads, 2,000+ paying users
*   Ramadan mode feature launch
*   "30 Days of Ihyaa" challenge
*   Partnership with mosques for community challenges
*   Zakat integration (donate percentage of subscription to verified charity)
*   Ramadan-specific content marketing
*   App Store featuring submission (Apple/Google love seasonal apps)
### Phase 4: Post-Ramadan Retention (Month 7-8)
**Target**: Retain 40% of Ramadan users as MAU
*   "Keep the momentum" campaign
*   Shawwal fasting tracker (6 days of Shawwal)
*   Habit transition plan (Ramadan habits → daily habits)
*   Re-engagement push notifications
*   Annual plan promotion (lock in Ramadan users at discounted rate)
### Phase 5: Steady State Growth (Month 9-12)
**Target**: 50,000 downloads, 2,000+ paying users
*   Family plan launch
*   Wearable integration
*   Referral program (give 1 month, get 1 month)
*   Corporate/institutional pilot (Islamic schools, companies with Muslim employees)
*   Localization: Arabic, Malay, Turkish (in order of priority)

## 9\. AI Operating Costs (Monthly Estimate)
Berapa biaya AI per bulan pada berbagai scale. Stack: GPT-5.6 Luna (primary) + DeepSeek V4.1-Flash (fallback) + Jev (decision layer) + embeddings.

| Scale | LLM (Luna + DeepSeek) | Jev | Embeddings | Total |
| ---| ---| ---| ---| --- |
| 1K DAU, 10 msgs/user/mo | ~$2-3 | ~$1 | ~$0.50 | ~$4-5 |
| 10K DAU, 10 msgs/user/mo | ~$16-32 | ~$10 | ~$5 | ~$31-47 |
| 50K DAU, 15 msgs/user/mo | ~$120-240 | ~$75 | ~$25 | ~$220-340 |

Dengan Rp49k/month per subscriber, AI cost per paying user di 10K DAU = ~$0.003-0.005/month. Negligible. AI bukan cost center yang signifikan, tapi infrastructure (hosting, database, push notifications) yang perlu diperhatikan.

**Fallback chain benefit**: Jika OpenAI down/rate-limit, automatic failover ke DeepSeek (different infrastructure). Zero downtime untuk AI coach. DeepSeek off-peak pricing (50% discount antara 01:00-04:00 dan 06:00-10:00 UTC weekdays) bisa dimanfaatkan untuk scheduled batch jobs (daily plan regeneration, weekly summaries).
