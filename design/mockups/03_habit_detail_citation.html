Physical

Brisk Walk to the Masjid

Step 2 of 4 · 15 min

A brisk 15-minute walk, ideally to the masjid for prayer. Pick up the pace slightly — enough that talking is still easy but singing isn't.

Why this habit — the evidence
Clinical evidence STRONG
Walking ~7,000 steps/day vs. 2,000 was linked to 47% lower all-cause mortality and 38% lower dementia risk.
Ding D, Nguyen B, Nau T, et al. (2025). The Lancet Public Health, 10(8):e668. Dose-response meta-analysis, 57 studies.
47%lower all-cause mortality, 7,000 vs 2,000 steps/day
GRADE certainty is moderate for most outcomes but very low for falls specifically — treat the falls figure with caution.
🔗 doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(25)00164-1
Rooted in sunnah
Hadith Sahih
"Whoever purifies himself in his house then walks to one of the houses of Allah to perform one of the obligatory prayers, one of his steps will erase a sin and the other will raise him a degree."
Sahih Muslim
